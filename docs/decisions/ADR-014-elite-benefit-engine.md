# ADR-014: Sourced, independent effective benefits

Accepted for Stage 8, 2026-10-12. Implements the approved brief §17 and ADR-004.

Keep a versioned, strict benefit bundle independent of the unchanged property catalog, pricing engines, scoring and saved version-1 planner. Resolve declarative selectors in a pure engine: property override → brand → status, with explicit null context and three-way condition matching. Unknown applicability blocks a lower-precedence assertion. Rules retain IDs, official source references, sections, confidence, verification, validity and disputes. Identical applicable outcomes agree; contradictory same-level claims or opposing official entitlements across layers produce unknown plus a visible conflict. An intended inclusion or clarification has a distinct basis from elite entitlement.

Do not infer resort or convention classification from a name, scenery, event space or absence of text. Catalog classifications require current matching evidence. Unknown classifications withhold guaranteed checkout and scope-dependent benefits. Reviews and policy claims require refresh after 180 days. Expired/future claims remain inspectable but cannot apply. Editorial observations never enter entitlement resolution.

Marketing an all-inclusive safari package does not establish membership in the separate All-Inclusive by Marriott Bonvoy program. Leave scope-dependent rules unknown at the three safari properties until that participation is confirmed. Display independently published meal inclusions. Admit current official rules for the six existing brands only. No catalog growth, account verification, rate changes or new scoring weights.

Use the saved manual profile status by default. A transient status preview does not write profile or URLs. Property selection and comparison context may appear in hash routes. Property details and comparison use live saved status. No persisted benefit result or migration is required; Stage 7 backups reconstruct unchanged. Nightly Upgrade Awards remain separate from status-based arrival upgrades.
