# ADR-015 — Reviewed catalog expansion

Status: Accepted for the explicitly approved Stage 9 only, 2026-10-12.

## Decision

Append 75 manually researched leisure/destination options to the 25-property seed in five disjoint batches of 15. Keep the six existing brands, stable IDs, strict domain schemas, static-first delivery and version-one planner unchanged. Each addition has current official identity/location evidence, neutral sourced experience classifications, a separate desk-review selection rationale and a dated official benefit review. New quality/service ratings, upgrade probabilities, prices and operating assumptions are not invented.

Retain the existing catalog admission and benefit resolution engines. Add an expansion audit command to the schema gate: reconstruct each cumulative batch, check catalog/benefit admission and source references, verify the entire Stage 8 baseline by canonical JSON SHA-256 and explicitly enumerate the reviewed production conflicts. This is a static maintainer gate, not a hotel retrieval or monitoring integration.

## Evidence treatment

The official Deer Valley page gives two accommodation counts; preserve both and withhold the snapshot. Al Bustan's open advisory and July renovation/closure notice disagree; retain the explicit open claim and an unknown disputed notice, without inferring a closed status or reopening date. The Athenee's Platinum+ lounge offering opposes the general Luxury Collection lounge exclusion in the unchanged conservative engine; preserve both policy traces and return unknown. The exact published Waikiki participation exclusions use policy-basis property overrides: they explicitly qualify the general program and are not invented evidence disputes.

Premium package products and free-breakfast advertising are distinct from elite entitlements. Domes Haute Living and Al Maha's separate program scope remain unknown. No event space is treated as convention classification. Historical/unknown/disputed evidence is never erased to meet the property-count target.

## Consequences

Exactly 100 properties are admitted; coverage is breadth of curated planning choices, not factual completeness or a quality ranking. Optional unknowns remain valid. Scores, trip totals, manual rate limits, certificate rules and planner JSON are unchanged. New data increases the static JS payload; gzip/build sizes are reported and browser coverage expands to all 100 detail/benefit routes. Stage 10 remains rejected by the manifest and unimplemented.
